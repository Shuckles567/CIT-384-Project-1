Johnny Storm (The Human Torch)

About Johnny Storm

Johnny Storm, also known as the Human Torch, is a fictional superhero appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. He is a member of the Fantastic Four.

Johnny Storm is known for his fiery personality and his ability to engulf his entire body in flames, fly, and control fire. He is often portrayed as the youngest and most impulsive member of the Fantastic Four, bringing energy and enthusiasm to the team.

Comic Book History

Johnny Storm first appeared in "The Fantastic 4" #1 in November 1961, created by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby. Over the years, he has become one of Marvel's most beloved characters, known for his catchphrase "Flame on!"

Powers and Abilities

  • Pyrokinesis: Johnny can generate and control fire, allowing him to create fireballs, streams of flame, and even engulf his entire body in flames.
  • Flight: By propelling himself with flames, Johnny can fly at high speeds.
  • Heat Resistance: He is immune to fire and heat, allowing him to withstand extreme temperatures without harm.
