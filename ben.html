Ben Grimm (The Thing)

About Ben Grimm

Ben Grimm, also known as The Thing, is a member of the Fantastic 4. He was a skilled pilot and astronaut before being exposed to cosmic rays during a scientific mission in space. As a result of the exposure, Ben's body transformed into a rock-like form, granting him superhuman strength and durability.

Despite his tough exterior, Ben is known for his loyalty and strong sense of justice. He often serves as the team's muscle and is always ready to protect his friends and fight against evil.

Comic Book History

Ben Grimm first appeared in "The Fantastic 4" #1 in November 1961, created by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby. Over the years, he has become one of Marvel's most beloved characters, known for his catchphrase "It's clobberin' time!"

Powers and Abilities

  • Superhuman Strength: Ben possesses immense physical strength, allowing him to lift heavy objects and overpower enemies.
  • Durability: His rock-like skin provides him with exceptional resistance to physical damage.
  • Endurance: Ben can withstand extreme conditions and recover from injuries that would incapacitate a normal human.
