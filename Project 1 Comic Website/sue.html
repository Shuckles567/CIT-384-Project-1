Sue Storm (The Invisible Woman)

About Sue Storm

Sue Storm, also known as The Invisible Woman, is a member of the Fantastic 4. She gained her powers after being exposed to cosmic rays during a scientific mission in space. Sue has the ability to become invisible and create force fields, making her a formidable member of the team.

Sue is known for her intelligence, compassion, and strong sense of responsibility. She often serves as the emotional anchor of the team and is deeply committed to protecting her loved ones and fighting for justice.

Comic Book History

Sue Storm first appeared in "The Fantastic 4" #1 in November 1961, created by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby. Over the years, she has become one of Marvel's most iconic female characters, known for her strength, courage, and leadership qualities.

Powers and Abilities

  • Invisibility: Sue can render herself and others invisible, allowing her to move unseen and avoid detection.
  • Force Fields: She can create powerful force fields that can protect herself and others from harm.
  • Telekinesis: In some versions of the comics, Sue has demonstrated limited telekinetic abilities, allowing her to manipulate objects with her mind.
